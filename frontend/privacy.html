Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This is a first draft, not a finished legal document. It has not been reviewed by an attorney. It must be reviewed by a licensed attorney before Attune is used to process real customer payments or handle customer/instructor data at scale.

1. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains what information Attune collects from customers and instructors, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what control you have over it. "Attune," "we," and "us" refer to the Attune platform described in our Terms of Service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the information you give us directly when you create and use an account:

DataWhy we collect it
Name, email, phone numberCreating your account, logging you in, and letting instructors/customers contact each other about a booking
Password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text) — not collected at all if you sign up with GoogleSecuring your account
Address / city / stateGeocoded to a location so we can match customers with nearby instructors and calculate travel distance
Booking and lesson-request history, package selections, notes you add to a requestRunning the matching and scheduling process, and showing you your own history
Reviews and ratings you write or receiveLetting customers rate instructors and helping other customers choose
Payment card details entered at checkout (card number, expiry, security code)See Section 3 — used only to process your booking

We also store certain account-standing information, like whether an admin has suspended an account, and reports or blocks you or another user file against each other, for trust-and-safety purposes.

3. Payments

Payment information you provide when booking a session (card number, expiry, and security code) is transmitted to our server to process your booking. It is used only for that purpose and is not stored in our database. Attune does not currently route payments through a live payment processor, so no real charge is made against your card today; this will change as the product matures, and this section will be updated when it does. We do not make any claim here about a specific security certification or encryption standard until real payment processing is in place.

4. Third Parties We Share Data With

We don't sell your personal information. We do share limited data with the services that help us run Attune:

  • Resend — sends transactional emails on our behalf (e.g. match notifications, booking updates, password resets). Resend receives the recipient's email address and the content of the message.
  • Google — if you choose "Sign in with Google," Google verifies your identity and shares your name and email with us to create or log you into your account. We don't receive your Google password.
  • Our hosting and database provider — our application and database run on third-party infrastructure (currently Render), which stores your account data on our behalf.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to investigate a report of abuse, fraud, or a safety concern involving our platform.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Attune doesn't use tracking cookies. When you log in, your session token is stored in your browser's localStorage so you stay logged in between visits. It's used to authenticate your requests to our API and isn't shared with any third party.

6. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You can view and edit most of your profile information directly in the app. You can request a copy of the data we hold about you by contacting us (Section 9).

Customers can permanently delete their account and associated data (bookings, lesson requests, reviews, recurring series) at any time from the app's account settings. Instructors can request account deletion by contacting us — self-service instructor account deletion isn't available in the app yet. Deleting your account removes the data described above from our production database; it doesn't retroactively remove copies that may exist in backups or in a third party's own records (e.g. an email already sent via Resend).

7. Data Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period afterward for legitimate business or legal purposes (e.g. resolving a trust-and-safety report), unless you request deletion sooner as described above.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Attune changes. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where practical, let you know in the app.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or want to exercise a data right described above? Email us at support@attune.app.
